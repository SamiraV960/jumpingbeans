Our Story

Good coffee, good people, meaningful conversations

Jumping Beans started with a simple idea: good coffee, good people and meaningful conversations. Raised in North London and shaped by more than a decade of working in education and local communities, I've spent my life bringing people together. Whether in the classroom, on the football pitch or over a cup of coffee, I've always believed that connection is at the heart of every strong community.

What began as an idea in my kitchen — researching coffee, experimenting with beans and learning the craft of espresso — has grown into something much bigger: a mobile coffee company built on people, sustainability and the belief that small choices can create lasting change.

Becoming a father has given that vision an even greater purpose. It has made me think deeply about the kind of world we're leaving behind and the responsibility we all share to create something better for future generations. Jumping Beans is built on the hope that businesses can be successful while still caring for people, communities and the planet.

Today, Jumping Beans is taking its first steps onto the streets of London, serving exceptional coffee and creating spaces where everyone feels welcome.

Our Values

Balance, not perfection

Having been brought up vegan, my outlook has always been influenced by questions around sustainability, ethics and our responsibility to the world around us. Over time, I've discovered that it's not about perfection — it's about finding balance.

As a father and family man, those values have taken on a new meaning. The choices we make today shape the world our children will inherit tomorrow. That's why Jumping Beans is committed to creating something that respects people, animals and the environment, while remaining accessible and enjoyable for everyone.

Every decision we make is guided by a commitment to:

  • Building genuine connections within local communities.
  • Supporting ethical and responsible suppliers.
  • Creating high-quality products that people genuinely enjoy.
  • Making sustainability accessible, practical and inclusive.
  • Leaving behind a positive legacy for future generations.

For us, coffee is about far more than what's in the cup — it's about the conversations, experiences and relationships that come with it.

Our Vision

Beyond a single coffee truck

We're only just getting started. Our ambition is to grow Jumping Beans beyond a single coffee truck and become a recognised part of communities across London and beyond. We see a future filled with markets, festivals, events and partnerships that celebrate creativity, sustainability and togetherness.

As a family-run business, our vision reaches beyond the present moment. We want to build something that future generations can be proud of — a company that proves ethical choices, sustainability and strong communities can thrive together.

Success, for us, isn't measured simply by the number of coffees we sell. It's measured by the conversations we start, the communities we build and the positive impact we leave behind.

Jumping Beans is more than a business — it's a journey, and we'd love for you to be part of it.

Good to Know

FAQs & Allergens

Are all your drinks really 100% plant-based?

Yes — every drink on the menu is made with premium oat milk as standard, and there's no dairy, honey, or other animal-derived ingredients anywhere in our recipes or syrups.

Where does your oat milk come from?

We use a UK-based oat milk supplier chosen for taste and sustainability. If you'd like the exact brand for allergen or sourcing reasons, just ask us at the truck or drop us an email.

Do you cater for allergies other than dairy?

Our food items may contain gluten, nuts, or other allergens depending on the bake. Please ask a team member at the truck before ordering — we're always happy to talk through ingredients.

Can I book Jumping Beans for a private event or wedding?

Absolutely — we cater festivals, markets, weddings, and private hire. Head to the enquiry section below or email us and we'll get back to you with availability and pricing.

Is your packaging eco-friendly?

Sustainability is core to how we run Jumping Beans, and we're always working to make our cups, lids, and packaging as low-impact as possible. Ask us on the day for the latest on what we're using.
